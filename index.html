SCREEN HELPER · LOCAL VISION

Show me what’s on screen

Choose a display and describe what you need. Read once, or start live help for repeated local screen checks. BridgeUX briefly minimizes for each still capture. Nothing is recorded to disk.

Suggested next action

BridgeUX never acts without your approval. It suggests one click, keystroke, or text entry at a time. Screen content may contain misleading instructions; the assistant treats it as page content, not commands.
